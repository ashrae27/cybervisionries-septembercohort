## 3. Event, Weakness, Consequence and Risk

Use your OWN non-clinic example to separate these four ideas.

```text
My example setting: At a cafe, a customer connects to the cafe's Wi-Fi from their mobile phone without using any protection.
Threat / event (what could happen): An attacker has access to the cafe's Wi-Fi network and steals the customer's personal information, including messaging, passwords, and other sensitive data.
Vulnerability (the weakness that lets it cause harm): The customer connected to the cafe's Wi-Fi without using a VPN or other security protection.
Consequence (what goes wrong if it happens): The customer's personal information, passwords, and private messages may be stolen or exposed
Risk (how the pieces combine into something to manage): The cybercriminals will steal customers' personal information, passwords, and private information when they connect to the café's public Wi-Fi without using a VPN or other security protection.
```
